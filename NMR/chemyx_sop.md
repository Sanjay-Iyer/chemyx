Protocol for the Si6 sequence:
1) Add catalyst and starting reagents together and mix in a 2-neck round-bottom flask under N2.
2) Withdraw 8 mL (needle out of solution)
3) Lower needle into solution
a) Withdraw 5 mL
4) Pause for 5 min (may be longer depending on how many NMR scans we want)
a) Run NMR
5) Infuse 13mL
          	a) Raise needle
6) Withdraw 5mL
7) Infuse 5mL
8) Repeat steps 2-7 every 60 minutes for ~24 hrs (or until reaction completion)
9) Add Diphenyl silane
10) Repeat steps 2-7 every 15 minutes for ~1.5 hrs (or until reaction completion)
11) Add Acetone
12) Repeat steps 2-7 every 15 minutes for ~1.5 hrs (or until reaction completion)
13) Add Diphenyl silane
14) Repeat steps 2-7 every 15 minutes for ~1.5 hrs (or until reaction completion)
15) Repeat steps 11-14 ___ (desired) number of times.
Stop the reaction
